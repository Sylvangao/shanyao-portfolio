ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Shanyao.

Independent product designer / Design & leadership

I’m a product designer with 13 years of experience across Tencent, Zhihu, Meizu and Baidu. Working across consumer products and enterprise services, I turn complex business logic into clear, effective experiences.

My practice spans user research, interaction and visual design, and brand identity. With five years of team management experience, I build products and design teams from the ground up, using research and data to inform decisions.

I enjoy breaking down problems and making systems understandable. I work closely with product and engineering, connecting business goals with the details that make an experience useful and coherent.
